📋 General Notes
  • 💨Short course but plays much harder than yardage — high winds, severe slopes, blind shots & fescue
  • 🌬️Wind mild in the morning, peaks ~3pm, generally off the ocean (west). Strongest on high holes: #1,2,6,11,17,18
  • ⛳Greens built into predominant slopes — trust green maps & Aimpoint, NOT your eyes
  • 🏌️Greens typically slow but severely sloped. Even 3–5ft putts often need to be played well outside the hole
  • 🌄Rarely a flat lie — uphill/downhill and above/below feet lies are the norm
  • 📊Rating: 71.1 / Slope: 132
